PanCode / Privacy

Privacy on pancode.dev

pancode.dev counts its visitors with Google Analytics. PanCode, the program you install, needs no account and sends nothing home. This page is about the website only.

What the site measures

The site uses Google Analytics 4. Its script loads from googletagmanager.com and reports to google-analytics.com. It records page views (the page address, its title and the page you came from) and three events: outbound_click when you follow a link to GitHub or npm, copy_install when you copy an install command, and theme_toggle when you switch between the dark and light themes.

Google signals and ad personalization are off, and advertising storage is always denied. The site runs no ads and no other tracker. GitHub Pages hosts the site and serves its requests under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Your choice

The site uses Google Consent Mode. Until you accept, analytics storage is denied: Google Analytics sets no cookies and receives only cookieless pings that carry no identifier for you. If you accept, it sets its first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_ followed by the site's id) so it can tell a returning visit from a new one.

The banner at the foot of the window asks once. Your answer stays in this browser's local storage under pancode-analytics-consent.

Change or withdraw it

Your current choice: not made yet.

Decline withdraws an earlier Accept: the site sets analytics storage back to denied and deletes the Google Analytics cookies on pancode.dev. Clearing this site's data in your browser also resets the choice, and the banner asks again.
